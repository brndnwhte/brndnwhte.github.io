Brendan White is a Bachelor of Philosophy candidate at the University of Pittsburgh studying mathematics, data science, and statistics. His research interests include quantitative approaches to social choice, game theory, conflict studies, and economics. Brendan is also a member of the Heinz Chapel Choir, an internationally-known a cappella ensemble, and a member of the board of directors for the Lutheran Campus Ministry in Greater Pittsburgh.
